# Public-source Reconstruction Method

OpenEUV uses a repeatable research loop:

1. **Anchor** — collect first-party public statements.
2. **Disclose** — locate relevant published patents and standards.
3. **Cross-check** — compare academic literature and independent models.
4. **Reconstruct** — create an original diagram/model that only claims what the evidence supports.
5. **Tag uncertainty** — mark assumptions, illustrative geometry and unknowns.
6. **Review** — invite domain contributors to challenge the evidence chain.

The goal is not to imitate proprietary CAD. The goal is a transparent engineering map that improves as public evidence accumulates.
